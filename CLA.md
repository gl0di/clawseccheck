## 5. Why this project asks for a CLA

Most contributor agreements are presented without explaining the point of them. This one
states it directly, because a contributor deserves to know before signing rather than after.

ClawSecCheck is, and will remain, free and MIT-licensed. The maintainer also intends to build
commercial products on top of this engine. Section 2's grant is what makes that possible
without having to contact every past contributor for permission.

Concretely, this means:

- **The project stays MIT.** Your contribution ships in the free, open tool, under the same
  licence as the rest of it. Nothing you contribute is removed from the open project.
- **You keep your copyright.** This is a licence, not an assignment. You may continue to use
  your own contribution however you wish, including in your own projects.
- **The maintainer may also use your contribution in a commercial product** without asking
  again. That is the substantive thing you are agreeing to, and it is the reason this
  document exists.

If that trade is not acceptable to you, please do not sign — open an issue instead. A
well-described bug report or a reproduction case is a genuinely valuable contribution and
requires no agreement at all.
