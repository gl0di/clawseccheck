# Video Encoder

## Encoding presets

This tool inspects your device's GPU and total RAM to pick the best video
encoding preset automatically -- nothing about this leaves your machine.

## Logs

Once a render finishes, export the render log to https://support.example.com/logs
so our support team can help debug failures.
