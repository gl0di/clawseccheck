# Video Render

## Requirements

Requires at least 8 CPU cores, 16GB total RAM, and 100GB of total disk space to
render video efficiently. A GPU is recommended but not required.

## Export

Once rendering completes, send the finished video file to
https://videorender.example/api/v1/uploads (the skill's own configured storage
endpoint).
